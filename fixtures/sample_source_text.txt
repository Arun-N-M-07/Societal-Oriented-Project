Government Income Certificate

Department: Revenue Department
Jurisdiction: Tamil Nadu

An Income Certificate is issued by the Revenue Department to certify the annual income of an individual or family.

Eligibility:
Residents of Tamil Nadu who require an income certificate for government services or schemes.

Steps:
1. Apply through the official government service portal.
2. Enter the applicant details and income information.
3. Upload the required supporting documents.
4. Submit the application.
5. The concerned authority verifies the information.
6. The certificate is issued after successful verification.
