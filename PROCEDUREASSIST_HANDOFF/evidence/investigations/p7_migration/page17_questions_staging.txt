## How do I edit my CAN details on e-Sevai?
   identify: matched [17] | guidance on 17: 10 claims, 9 CAN-editing
   CAN Go to https://www.tnesevai.tn.gov.in/ on your web browser.
   CAN In the Sign In section, click on "Citizen Login".
   CAN Enter the login credentials.
   CAN Correctly enter the Captcha Code (Not case sensitive) and click on "Login".
   CAN In the "Services" tab, select "DEPARTMENT WISE" and then click on "Revenue Department".
   CAN Click on "REV-101 Community Certificate". This will open a new browser tab.
   CAN In this Search section, look for your CAN using any of the six fields provided.
       Once found, select it by clicking the Radio button. This will auto-fill the "Mobile Number" field at the bottom.
   CAN Click on "Generate OTP" and then enter the OTP that has been sent to your registered phone number. Click on "Confirm OTP" once done.
   CAN Now you will be able to see the "Edit CAN Detail" button. Click it and you will be able to update your CAN details.
